A New York house with a British silhouette.

NEW YORK EST. 2019

Pharoah began in 2019 with custom suits. Today the house makes ready-to-wear tailoring, luxury streetwear and accessories, inspired by classic British silhouettes, with creativity and edge at the heart of every design.

Collections are released in limited runs, at exclusive events around the world. On February 12, the house brings that to Los Angeles, and your brand can stand beside it.

Pharoah Kirk

Founder and creative director of Pharoah

Pharoah Kirk learned fabric before he learned fashion. His parents ran a dry-cleaning business, and the textiles that passed through it led him to the designers who shaped him: Tracy Reese, Dapper Dan, Giorgio Armani.

He started the house to give athletes a better image in the media, building their brands one suit at a time. His first athlete client was Seth Curry. On the 2019 NFL Draft red carpet, Daniel Jones and Devin White wore Pharoah. Today he styles NFL players for game day.

“…when they choose Black designers it redefines who luxury is for.”

Pharoah Kirk, in Forbes, 2026
